# How Recce maps to the judging criteria

**Technical feasibility.** A working, deployable prototype: FastAPI plus Gemini (structured output and vision), Google Places and Street View, a custom route optimizer, real sun math, and a React/Leaflet UI, served as one Cloud Run container. Demo mode runs the full flow with no keys.

**Innovation and novelty.** A script-to-shoot-ready agent. The novel pieces: Gemini scores each candidate's real photography against a director's brief on look and mood (not keyword search), ranking a wide pool so genuine matches rise and lookalikes fall away; a scout-day route is optimized over the shortlist; and golden-hour windows are computed per location and date.

**Real-world applicability.** Location scouting is a real, costly, manual workflow. The output is concrete: a scored shortlist with rationales and a shoot-day packet a crew could actually use.

**Market potential and fundability.** Clear B2B buyers with real budgets (film, TV, commercials, branded content), an indie and agency wedge, and an expansion path into a rights-cleared location marketplace. Seat-based SaaS plus usage credits.

**Go-to-market traction.** The product demos in 60 seconds and films beautifully: a map filling with AI-matched locations and concept frames. The ICP is crisp (location managers, line producers, indie filmmakers, ad-agency producers) and entry is low-friction through demo mode.

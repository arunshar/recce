"THE LONG WAY DOWN" — sample scenes

INT. SALTWATER DINER — DAWN

Chrome and cracked vinyl. A row of empty booths catches the first grey light
through fogged windows. Steam rises off a coffee urn nobody is watching. RENA
(40s, raincoat still dripping) sits alone at the counter, a revolver wrapped in
a dish towel beside her cup. The neon "OPEN" sign buzzes, half dead. Outside,
the ocean is a flat sheet of pewter.

RENA
(to no one)
Low tide. Everything it took, it has to give back eventually.

A bell over the door. She does not turn around.


EXT. CLIFFSIDE LIGHTHOUSE — DUSK

A whitewashed lighthouse on a wind-scoured headland, the last orange light
raking across wet grass. Far below, surf hammers black rocks. RENA climbs the
narrow path, coat snapping in the gale. The lamp above begins its slow turn,
sweeping the darkening water.


EXT. RAIN-SOAKED ALLEY — NIGHT

A tight brick alley behind a shuttered theater, puddles trembling with
reflected marquee light. Fire escapes drip. A single sodium lamp throws long
shadows. RENA presses into a doorway as headlights crawl past the alley mouth,
then keeps moving deeper into the dark.
